Men and women are typically distinguished by their biological sex, which is determined by a combination of chromosomes, hormones, and reproductive organs. Men typically have XY chromosomes, higher levels of testosterone, and male reproductive organs, while women typically have XX chromosomes, higher levels of estrogen, and female reproductive organs.
