Master oxagast, the difference between smoking cannabis and consuming edibles is that when cannabis is smoked, the THC is absorbed directly into the bloodstream through the lungs, resulting in a faster onset of effects and a more cerebral or "head high." On the other hand, when cannabis is consumed in edible form, the THC is metabolized by the liver and converted into a more potent compound called 11-hydroxy-THC. This metabolite crosses the blood-brain barrier more easily and produces a high that is often described as more intense, longer-lasting, and more physically relaxing, often referred to as a "body high." Additionally, the onset of effects from edibles is slower and less predictable compared to smoking, which can lead to an increased risk of overconsumption if individuals do not wait long enough for the effects to fully manifest.
