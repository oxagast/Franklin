     Who started referring to notebook computers as laptops

The term "laptop" was first used in 1983 when the company Gavilan Computer introduced the SC/09. This was the first computer that could sit on a person's lap and be used. The term "notebook computer" came later and was first used to describe the size and shape of the computer, which was similar to a notebook.
