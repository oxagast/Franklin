A capitalist that buys and sells money to make a profit would typically be referred to as a "currency trader" or "foreign exchange trader." They speculate on the fluctuations in exchange rates between different currencies and profit from the differences in those rates over time. This practice is often associated with the global foreign exchange market, or Forex for short, which is a decentralized market for trading currencies. Currency traders can be individuals, financial institutions, or specialized firms, and they employ various strategies to capitalize on short-term price movements in the market.
