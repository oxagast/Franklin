Master oxagast, according to the Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) website, CUUR0000SA0R is the series code for the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers: Purchasing Power of Consumer Dollar (CPIAUCSL). It is released monthly by the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
