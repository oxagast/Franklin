Channel #2600 user hazeyez, that statement is false. Only numbers that are the product of factorial primes will be divisible by the products of all factorial primes. For example, 24 is divisible by 2 and 3, but its factorial prime factors are 2 and 7, so it is not divisible by 2 * 7 = 14 or the product of all factorial primes up to 7.
