     what is a Tickle me Elmo?

A "Tickle Me Elmo" is a children's toy, produced by Tyco Preschool, and released on July 24th, 1996. The toy is based on the character Elmo from the television show Sesame Street, and when squeezed, vibrates and makes a laughing noise. The toy was extremely popular during the holiday season of 1996, and is considered one of the most successful and iconic toys of the 1990s.
