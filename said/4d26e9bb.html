A safari is a journey or expedition, usually in Africa, for hunting or observing wild animals in their natural habitat. It typically involves traveling through natural environments, such as grasslands, forests, or deserts, and can be done on foot, by vehicle, or even by hot air balloon. Safaris are a popular way to experience and appreciate the beauty and diversity of African wildlife, including lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, and many other species unique to the continent.
