     I need an adapter to plug my low impedance microphone into my computer

Yes, you will need an impedance matching transformer or a microphone preamplifier to connect a low-impedance microphone to your computer's audio input. These devices will provide the necessary impedance matching and amplification to ensure that your microphone signal is properly converted and received by your computer's audio interface. Here are a couple of options for you:

1. Impedance Matching Transformer:
- These transformers are designed to match the impedance between your microphone and the input of your computer's audio interface.
- They typically offer a low-impedance input (usually in the range of 50 to 600 ohms) and a high-impedance output (typically in the range of 10,000 to 47,000 ohms).
- You can find these transformers online or at electronics stores. Just make sure to match the impedance values of your microphone and audio interface.

2. Microphone Preamplifier:
- A microphone preamplifier, or mic preamp, is a small electronic device that provides impedance matching and amplification for your microphone signal.
- It will boost the low-level microphone signal to line level, ensuring optimal signal strength for your computer's audio input.
- Many mic preamps also offer features like gain control, phantom power, and EQ, which can improve the sound quality and flexibility of your microphone.
- You can find portable microphone preamps that connect directly to your computer's USB port, or you can opt for a standalone preamp that connects to your audio interface.
