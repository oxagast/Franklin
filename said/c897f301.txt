In the season two premiere of Star Trek: The Original Series, "The Man Trap," which aired on September 16, 1966, the Enterprise visits the planet M113 to check on a team of archaeologists, including Professor Robert Crater, his wife Nancy, and their assistant, Lieutenant Commander Jack Daley. They discover that Nancy has been replaced by a shape-shifting creature called a Salt Vampire, which kills by extracting salt from its victims. The creature had taken on Nancy's form to lure men to their deaths, and the real Nancy was already dead. The episode explores themes of deception, greed, and the value of human life.
