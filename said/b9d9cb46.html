     What is fungible

Fungibility refers to the interchangeability of individual items or elements within a larger group or class. In simpler terms, it means that items are identical or nearly identical in all aspects that matter and can be easily substituted for one another.

A common example used to illustrate fungibility is money. Most forms of currency are considered fungible because one unit is equivalent to another. For instance, any $10 bill can be exchanged for another $10 bill without any loss or gain in value. They are interchangeable and hold the same worth.
