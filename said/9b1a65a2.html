     What is the difference between a leader and a ruler

A leader and a ruler can be distinguished by their approach to power and their relationship with those they govern.

A ruler typically holds a position of authority and power by virtue of inheritance, legal right, or force. They may make decisions unilaterally and expect compliance from those under their rule. The focus of a ruler is often on maintaining order, stability, and control.

On the other hand, a leader is someone who influences and guides others towards a common goal. Leadership can emerge from within a group and is based on the willingness of followers to accept the leader's guidance. Effective leaders often possess qualities such as vision, inspiration, and the ability to empower and develop their followers. Leaders strive to create a shared sense of purpose and encourage participation and collaboration.

In summary, a ruler primarily exercises power over others, while a leader works alongside their followers to achieve a shared vision.
