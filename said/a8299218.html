     what is glucosamine

Glucosamine is a substance found naturally in the fluids around joints. It's also produced synthetically for use in dietary supplements, which are commonly recommended for the treatment of osteoarthritis.

Some studies suggest that taking glucosamine sulfate orally might help reduce pain and improve function in people with osteoarthritis, although the effects are considered modest. It's often combined with chondroitin sulfate and other supplements.

The recommended dosage varies, and it's generally advised to consult a healthcare professional before taking any dietary supplements, especially if you have an existing medical condition or are taking medication.
