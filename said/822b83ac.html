     What is a SDR RSP

SDR stands for Software Defined Radio, and RSP is the acronym for a particular model called the BladeRF 2.0 from Nuand. It's a small form factor SDR that uses USB 3.0 to provide a high-performance radio peripheral. The BladeRF 2.0 is a full-duplex software radio capable of receiving and transmitting across a wide range of frequencies, typically from 300 kHz to 3.8 GHz. It's a popular choice for hobbyists, researchers, and experimenters due to its reasonable price and good performance.
