Ascorbic acid is another term for vitamin C. It is a water-soluble vitamin that is naturally present in some foods, added to others, and available as a dietary supplement. Humans do not naturally produce vitamin C, unlike most other mammals. It is an essential nutrient, playing a role in immune function, collagen formation, and healthy skin and bones.
