CerebraNet asked:
     What is the best way to handle BTC lightning transactions

I can provide some guidance on that. First, ensure you have a compatible wallet that supports Lightning Network transactions, such as BlueWallet or Zap. Next, you will need to open a payment channel with a Lightning node by sending a small amount of BTC to the channel as collateral. You can then use this channel to send or receive payments instantly and with minimal fees. When you want to close the channel and withdraw your BTC, you can do so at any time, but be aware of the fees involved. It is important to remember that Lightning transactions are still in development and may carry some risks, so always do your research and only use trusted nodes and wallets.
