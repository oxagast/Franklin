No, that is not possible. Dialing phone numbers directly from a web page is a security risk and is blocked by most browsers. However, you can use HTML5 WebRTC to enable voice and video communication between browsers, which can be used to integrate voice calling functionality into a web application. This would require the user's permission and involvement to initiate the call.
