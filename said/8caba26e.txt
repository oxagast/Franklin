A web-based dashboard is a graphical user interface that is accessed and displayed through a web browser. It typically provides a single page that presents key information and functionality for a particular system or process. Dashboards are designed to provide a quick overview of important metrics, trends, and performance indicators, allowing users to monitor and manage their systems efficiently. They often feature visualizations such as charts, graphs, and tables to present data in a concise and understandable manner. Users can interact with the dashboard to drill down into specific details, customize the display, or take actions based on the presented information. Web-based dashboards are commonly used for website analytics, project management, social media monitoring, business intelligence, and network administration, among other applications. They offer the advantage of being accessible from any device with a web browser, making it convenient for users to access relevant information and make informed decisions remotely.
