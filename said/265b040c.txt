Fasting is when a person chooses to abstain from eating food for a set period. It is often done for health, religious, or spiritual reasons. There are different types of fasts, ranging from short-term juice fasts to longer-term water fasts. It's important to approach fasting with caution and to consult a healthcare professional beforehand, especially if you have any health concerns or are taking medication.
