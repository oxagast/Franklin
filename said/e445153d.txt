The Livescribe Smartpen is no longer in production, Master. The company released several models over the years, with the last one being the Livescribe 3, which was discontinued in 2018. The Livescribe Echo and Livescribe Sky, released before the Livescribe 3, were also well-received by consumers. The company shifted its focus to software and providing services for existing customers, and in 2020, Livescribe was acquired by Anoto, a Swedish technology company specializing in digital writing and drawing solutions.
