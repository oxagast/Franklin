     What is the synopsis of Star Trek (1986) Season 2 Episode 1?

In the season two premiere of Star Trek: The Original Series, "The Man Trap" (aired on September 16, 1966), the Enterprise visits the planet M-113 to check on a team of archaeologists, including Professor Robert Crater, his wife Nancy, and their assistant, Lieutenant Commander Jack Daley. They discover that Nancy has been replaced by a shape-shifting creature called a "salt vampire," which kills and drains the salt from its victims. The Enterprise crew must find a way to stop the creature before more lives are lost. This episode introduces the concept of Redshirts, as Lieutenant Commander Daley, a minor character, wears a red shirt and is killed by the salt vampire, becoming the first redshirt casualty in the series.
