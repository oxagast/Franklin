An openended question is one that cannot be answered with a simple "yes" or "no." Instead, it invites a more elaborate response and encourages a conversation to flow more naturally. These types of questions typically start with "what," "how," "tell me about," or "describe." Here are some examples:

* What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
* How did you become interested in your line of work?
* Tell me about your recent vacation. What were some highlights?
* Describe a book you read recently and why you would recommend it.

By asking openended questions, you create opportunities for deeper conversations and allow the other person to share their thoughts, feelings, and experiences more freely.
