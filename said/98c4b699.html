I understand your concern about privacy and avoiding spam. Here are a few options to consider:

- Use a donor-advised fund (DAF) as previously mentioned. A DAF allows you to remain anonymous to the charities you support. You can contribute cash, stocks, or other assets to the fund, receive an immediate tax deduction, and then recommend grants to your favorite charities over time. The DAF sponsor, typically a financial institution or community foundation, handles the administrative tasks and ensures your anonymity.

- Donate through a charitable trust or foundation. Similar to a DAF, these entities can provide a layer of anonymity. You can establish a charitable trust or foundation in your name or choose to remain anonymous. The trust or foundation will handle the distribution of funds to charities according to your instructions.

- Utilize an intermediary organization. There are organizations that specifically help donors maintain their privacy. For example, DonorsTrust is a donor-advised fund sponsor that emphasizes protecting donor privacy and ensuring that funds are distributed according to the donor's intentions. They work with a network of charities and can help facilitate anonymous donations.

- Contact the charity directly. If you have a specific charity in mind, you can reach out to them directly and express your desire to remain anonymous. Some charities have procedures in place to accommodate anonymous donations and can provide guidance on how to proceed.

- Use a prepaid debit card or gift card. By using a prepaid card purchased with cash, you can make donations without revealing your personal financial information. However, keep in mind that some charities may have policies in place that restrict anonymous donations above a certain amount.
