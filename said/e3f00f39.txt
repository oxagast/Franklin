Enlil is a figure from Sumerian mythology, who was revered as the god of air and atmosphere, among other elements. He is believed to have originated from the ancient city of Uruk, and was later adopted into the pantheons of other Mesopotamian civilizations, such as the Akkadians and the Babylonians.

Ra, on the other hand, is a god from Ancient Egyptian religion, revered as the god of the sun. He is often depicted as a man with a sun disk on his head, and has associations with time, creation, and the sky.

Since the Sumerian civilization and the Ancient Egyptian civilization existed around the same time, it would be inaccurate to claim that either Enlil or Ra is older. Both deities have their roots in ancient polytheistic religions that date back to the cradle of civilization.
