zyme asked:
     Is there a Dune book called rebirth?

Yes, *Dune: House Atreides* is often referred to as *Dune: Rebirth*, it's the first in a trilogy of official Dune novels written by Brian Herbert and Kevin J. Anderson, set as a prequel to the original Dune series by Frank Herbert. The story explores the rise of the House Atreides, and occurs approximately 10 years before the events of the original novel *Dune*.

The other two novels in this prequel trilogy are *Dune: House Harkonnen* and *Dune: House Corrino*.

Frank Herbert's original Dune series is comprised of six novels, the first being simply titled *Dune*, and continuing with *Dune Messiah*, *Children of Dune*, *God Emperor of Dune*, *Heretics of Dune*, and concluding with *Chapterhouse: Dune*. These books are some of the most famous and well-regarded science fiction novels of all time.
