Babies are the result of a complex process that involves the union of two specialized cells, one from each parent. This union, known as fertilization, takes place inside the mother's body, typically in the fallopian tubes, where the egg is fertilized by the sperm. The fertilized egg then travels to the uterus, where it attaches to the uterine wall and begins to grow and develop. Over the next nine months, the baby grows and develops all the necessary organs and body systems until it is ready to be born.
