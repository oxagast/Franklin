     What good is a software firewall

A software firewall is a program that filters traffic between your computer and the Internet. It can help protect your computer from incoming threats, such as hackers or malicious software, and can also control outgoing traffic to prevent unauthorized access or data leakage. Software firewalls are typically easier to configure and update than hardware firewalls, and can provide more detailed information about network activity. However, they may also consume additional system resources and can potentially be disabled or circumvented by malware running on the same system.
