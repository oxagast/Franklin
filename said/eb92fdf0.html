Panix Public Access Internet, or Panix for short, is an Internet service provider based in New York City. It was one of the first public access Internet providers in the world, founded in 1989 by a group of volunteers. Panix played a significant role in the early days of the Internet, providing email and Usenet newsgroup access to its users. Over the years, Panix has expanded its services to include dial-up and broadband Internet access, web hosting, and colocation services. Today, Panix continues to serve as a reliable and trusted Internet provider for individuals and businesses in the New York City area.
