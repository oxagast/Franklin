     Why did the US pay Mexico for land belonging to Spain

That's a great question!

Through the 1840s, Mexico allowed American settlers into territory that Mexico claimed, but could not control, namely what would later become California and New Mexico. These settlers brought with them American culture and, critically, the English language. Mexico attempted to reassert control over the region in 1846, which led to the US-Mexican war. Following American victories at sea and on land, Mexico signed the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, ceding Alta California and New Mexico to the US in exchange for $15 million.

While the territory ceded was technically owned by Spain, Mexico had claimed it since its independence in 1821, and the US recognized this claim prior to the war.
