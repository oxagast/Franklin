Yes, potatoes can be slow cooked. However, it is important to note that the texture and taste of slow-cooked potatoes can be quite different from those that are boiled, baked, or fried. Slow cooking potatoes can result in a softer, more creamy texture, and the extended cooking time can also cause the potatoes to take on a more intense flavor from the other ingredients in the slow cooker. It is generally recommended to cut potatoes into smaller pieces or thin slices before slow cooking to ensure even cooking and to avoid ending up with hard, undercooked potatoes. Additionally, adding a small amount of acidic ingredients, such as lemon juice or vinegar, can help keep the potatoes from turning brown or mushy during the extended cooking time.
