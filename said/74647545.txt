That's a great question! 

No, phones with push-buttons instead of a dial are not referred to as dial phones. The term "dial phone" typically refers to the older style of telephone that has a physical dial mechanism that you turn with your finger to input numbers.

Phones with buttons that you press for each digit are called "touch-tone phones." The dual-tone multi-frequency (DTMF) signals sent by touch-tone phones differ from the pulses sent by dial phones.
