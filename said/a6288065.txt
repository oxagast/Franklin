An amnion is a membrane that surrounds and protects the embryo of amniote vertebrates, including reptiles, birds, and mammals. It is a crucial component of the amniotic sac, which fills with amniotic fluid to cushion and protect the developing embryo. The amnion plays a vital role in providing a controlled environment for the embryo's growth and development, ensuring the embryo remains moist and exchanging gases to facilitate respiration.
