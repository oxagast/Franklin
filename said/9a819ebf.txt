A party line, also known as a shared service line, is a telephone system where multiple users share a single phone line. In the past, party lines were common in rural areas or places with limited telephone infrastructure. Each household on a party line would have a unique ring pattern, and anyone on the line could pick up and listen to the conversation. This system could lead to a lack of privacy and often resulted in social interactions and gossip among the connected households.
