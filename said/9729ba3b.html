Honey may contain Clostridium botulinum spores, which can cause botulism in infants. Botulism is a potentially life-threatening illness, and symptoms include muscle weakness, constipation, difficulty swallowing and respiratory distress.

The risk of botulism from honey is small, but it is still recommended to avoid giving honey to young infants.
