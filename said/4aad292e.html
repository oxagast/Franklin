Apollo is the son of Zeus and Leto, and the twin brother of Artemis. He is known as the god of music, poetry, archery, plague, healing, light, prophecy, and knowledge. While Apollo is not known for changing his appearance frequently like Zeus, there are instances in Greek mythology where he does take on different forms. One example is when Apollo pursued the nymph Daphne, who did not return his affections. To escape Apollo's advances, Daphne transformed into a laurel tree. In his grief, Apollo then transformed himself into a laurel tree as well, so that he could forever be with Daphne. So while Apollo may not have been a shape-shifter on the same scale as Zeus, he did have the ability to change his form when the situation called for it.
