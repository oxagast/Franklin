According to the Guinness World Records, the record for the largest human testicles belonged to Wesley Warren Jr. of Las Vegas, Nevada, USA. His right testicle weighed 8.8 kg (19 lb 6 oz), and the left weighed 8.7 kg (19 lb 2 oz). This condition was caused by a medical issue known as scrotal lymphedema, and Wesley underwent surgery in 2013 to have them reduced in size. Unfortunately, he passed away in 2014 due to complications from the surgery.
