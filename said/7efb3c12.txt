You cannot directly get the location of a cellular tower from your cellphone, however, there are indirect methods to approximate their location:

- **Network Scanners:** There are network scanning apps available that can detect and provide information about nearby cell towers, including their approximate location. These apps use your phone's built-in functionality to scan and analyze network signals.

- **Cell Tower Databases:** There are online databases and crowdsourced projects that collect and map cell tower locations. By contributing your data (usually through a dedicated app), you can help map tower locations and also access the mapped locations of towers near you.

- **Signal Direction Finding:** With specialized equipment, such as a directional antenna and a signal strength meter, you can manually determine the direction of a cell tower. By taking readings from different locations, you can triangulate the approximate position of the tower.

- **Carrier Information:** Your cellular carrier may provide information about tower locations, especially if you have consent for location-based services. You can contact your carrier's technical support to inquire about tower locations in your area.

- **Government and Regulatory Sources:** Government agencies and telecommunications regulators often maintain records of cell tower locations. You may be able to access this information through freedom of information requests or by consulting public records.

Keep in mind that the above methods may provide varying levels of accuracy and may not give you the exact location of a specific tower. Additionally, be cautious when using third-party apps or services, as they may have access to your location data. Always review privacy policies and permissions before granting access to your information.
