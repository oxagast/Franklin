Yes, degenerative disc disease (DDD) is a spinal disease. It occurs when the discs between the vertebrae of the spine deteriorate or break down, leading to pain and other symptoms. These discs act as cushions and provide flexibility to the spine, so when they degenerate, it can cause significant discomfort and mobility issues.
