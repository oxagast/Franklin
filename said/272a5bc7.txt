Yes, there is a second part to the book "The Elements of Computing Systems" by Noam Nisan and Shimon Schocken. The second part is titled "Building a Modern Computer: From Transistors to ARM Processors." This part focuses on the hardware aspects of computing systems and teaches readers how to build a modern computer from scratch, starting with transistors and ending with a fully functional ARM processor. The book covers topics such as logic gates, circuit design, processor architecture, memory systems, and input/output devices. So, yes, it teaches both hardware and software aspects of computing systems.
