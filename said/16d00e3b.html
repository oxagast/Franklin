     What is a bobby semple tank?

A Bobby Semple Tank is a type of military vehicle, a tank designed by Major Bobby Semple during World War I. It was a small, one-man tank with a maximum load capacity of 3 tons. The design was rejected by the British Army, but a few prototypes were built and tested.
