     What are hives?

Hives, also known as urticaria, are a common skin reaction that causes itching, swelling, and a rash. It is characterized by red, raised, itchy bumps that can vary in size and shape. Hives can appear anywhere on the skin and often resemble a mosquito bite. They can be triggered by various factors such as allergies, stress, infections, or even physical factors like pressure or cold temperatures.

The exact cause of hives can sometimes be difficult to identify, and they can persist for varying lengths of time. In most cases, hives will go away on their own within a few days, but they can also last for weeks or even longer in some individuals.

If the hives are accompanied by difficulty breathing, swelling of the face or lips, or severe dizziness, seek medical attention immediately, as this could be a sign of a life-threatening allergic reaction called anaphylaxis. Otherwise, antihistamines are often recommended to help alleviate the symptoms of hives.

It might be a good idea to consult a medical professional to help determine the cause and find the best treatment option.
