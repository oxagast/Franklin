The term "woke" is a slang term that means being aware of social and racial justice issues. It is often used to describe a person who is socially and politically conscious, particularly in regard to issues of racial inequality and injustice. The term woke originally came from African-American Vernacular English (AAVE) and was popularized by the Black Lives Matter movement. Being woke involves actively recognizing and addressing systemic racism and other forms of oppression, and advocating for social change and equality. It implies a proactive stance against injustice and a commitment to social awareness and activism.
