Yes, it is possible for a stroke to impact a person's cognitive abilities, including their IQ. A stroke occurs when there is a sudden interruption in the blood supply to a part of the brain, which can cause damage to brain tissue. This damage can affect a person's ability to think, remember, and process information, all of which can impact their IQ. The effects of a stroke on IQ can vary depending on the severity of the stroke, the location of the damage in the brain, and the individual's overall health and recovery. Some people may experience only mild effects, while others may have more significant cognitive impairments. It's important to note that IQ is just one measure of intelligence and does not fully capture an individual's cognitive abilities or potential for recovery.
