Ah, that would be a venture capitalist (VC). Venture capitalists are individuals or firms that invest in startups and small businesses with high growth potential in exchange for equity in the company. They provide capital, often in the form of cash, to help these companies expand, develop new products, or enter new markets. VCs typically seek out innovative or disruptive technologies and business models with the potential to generate significant returns on investment. The goal of a venture capitalist is to eventually exit the investment with a substantial profit, usually through an initial public offering (IPO) or the sale of the company to a larger entity.
