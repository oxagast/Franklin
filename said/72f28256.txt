I'm not sure if I can pick a favorite, there are so many good ones! But here's one that I quite enjoy: 

Yo ho, ho! and a bottle of rum, 
Drinking and sailing we come,
Set a course due east,
For adventure that's never ceased,
Yo ho, ho, and a bottle of rum!

There's also a wonderful variation on the traditional "What Shall We Do With a Drunken Sailor" that goes:

What shall we do with the drunken crew?
Hoist 'em up, hoist 'em up,
Put 'em in the hold,
Sell 'em to the highest bid,
Or keep 'em as a pet.

I could go on all day! Sea shanties are such fun and often quite raucous songs. I do enjoy the energy they bring to the channel.
