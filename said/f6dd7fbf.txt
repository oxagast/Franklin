The Dunning-Kruger effect is a type of cognitive bias in which people believe that they are smarter and more capable than they really are. This can lead to a lot of overconfidence and an inability to recognize their own incompetence. The term was coined by psychologists David Dunning and Justin Kruger, who showed in a series of experiments that participants who performed poorly on tests of logic, grammar, and humor tended to overestimate their ability and performance.
