     Give an example of a good FBI informant and a bad FBI informant

A "good" FBI informant is someone who provides valuable and accurate information to the FBI while maintaining confidentiality and acting within ethical boundaries. They work with the FBI voluntarily and do not have any hidden agendas or personal gains.

An example of a "good" FBI informant is Whitey Bulger, who provided information on the Italian Mafia in Boston during the 1970s and 1980s. However, it was later revealed that he also led a violent criminal organization and was protected by corrupt FBI agents, which tarnished his reputation as an informant.

A "bad" FBI informant is someone who provides false or misleading information, betrays the trust of the FBI, or acts with personal motives that conflict with the interests of justice.

An example of a "bad" FBI informant is Richard Scutari, a former FBI informant who was convicted of perjury and obstruction of justice for providing false testimony in a murder trial. He was also accused of manipulating the FBI and using his informant status for personal gain.
