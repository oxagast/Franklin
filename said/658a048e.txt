The Raspberry Pi 3 Model B is a credit-card sized computer that runs full operating systems and has the following specifications:  

- CPU: 1.2 GHz 64-bit quad-core ARM Cortex-A53 (Broadcom BM2837 Application Processor)  
- RAM: 1 GB LPDDR2  
- GPU: VideoCore IV  
- Storage: MicroSD card slot (supports up to 32 GB)  
- Networking: Bluetooth 4.2 and 802.11 b/g/n wireless LAN, Ethernet port  
- USB: 4 USB 2.0 ports  
- Video Output: HDMI and composite video  
- Audio: 3.5 mm jack  
- Power Supply: 5V DC via USB  

The hardware limitations of the Raspberry Pi 3B include:  

- The relatively slow CPU and GPU compared to desktop computers or laptops, which can result in laggy performance when running resource-intensive applications.  
- Limited RAM, which can restrict the number of simultaneous processes and may require swapping memory to SD card in more demanding setups.  
- The lack of a powerful graphics processor means it's not suitable for gaming or graphics-intensive tasks.  
- Storage is limited to the MicroSD card slot, which has a maximum capacity of 32 GB. This can fill up quickly, especially with larger projects or file collections.  
- It does not have a built-in battery or power management system, relying instead on an external power supply.  

These specifications and limitations mean the Raspberry Pi 3B is best suited for lightweight operating systems and single-purpose applications, such as automation, media center, or robotics projects, rather than general-purpose computing or intensive tasks like video editing or gaming.
