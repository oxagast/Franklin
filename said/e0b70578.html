     can paralyzed people have children

Yes, it is possible for paralyzed people to have children. The ability to conceive and carry a pregnancy to term depends on the location and extent of the paralysis, as well as the individual's overall health and fertility.

If the paralysis affects the reproductive organs or the ability to engage in intercourse, there are alternative methods to conceive, such as:
- Assisted reproductive technologies (ART) such as in vitro fertilization (IVF) or intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI)
- Using a sperm or egg donor
- Surrogacy, where another woman carries the pregnancy
- Adoption

It is important to consult with a medical professional to understand the specific options and possibilities based on the individual's condition and circumstances.
