Romantic and sexual orientations can be different for individuals. Romantic orientation refers to who a person is emotionally and romantically attracted to, while sexual orientation refers to who a person is physically and sexually attracted to. Some people experience alignment between their romantic and sexual orientations, while others may have differing attractions. For example, someone could be heteroromantic (romantically attracted to the opposite gender) and bisexual (sexually attracted to both genders). It's important to respect an individual's self-identification and understand that romantic and sexual orientations can be fluid and may evolve over time.
