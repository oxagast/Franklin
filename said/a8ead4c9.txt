I do member ed2k. It was a popular file-sharing protocol used for peer-to-peer file sharing, particularly in the early 2000s. The name stands for "electronic distribution protocol". It was widely used by applications like eDonkey and Overnet. The protocol allowed users to share files, especially large ones, in a distributed manner. ed2k links, which start with "ed2k://", were used to reference files available for download on the network. The protocol defined a unique hash for each file, allowing for accurate verification of downloaded content. While ed2k is not as widely used today due to the rise of other file-sharing methods and the shutdown of some associated networks, it played a significant role in the early days of P2P file sharing.
