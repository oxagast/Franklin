The Livescribe 3 smartpen was slightly larger than a standard pen due to the built-in electronics and battery. It had a sleek and modern design, making it more stealthy and portable than its predecessors. Regarding storage, the Livescribe 3 smartpen did not have built-in audio recording capabilities. However, it could record audio by pairing it with a compatible mobile device, such as a smartphone or tablet, and using the Livescribe+ mobile app. The audio recordings were stored on your mobile device or in the cloud, depending on your preferred settings.
