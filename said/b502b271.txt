Avocado plants should be placed outside after the last spring frost. As mentioned earlier, the timing varies across the state. For the lower elevations, this occurs around mid-March, so around this time would be ideal for planting avocado plants. Make sure to keep an eye on the weather forecast to ensure you don't miss the window and lose your plant!

Additionally, North Carolina's growing zone is primarily 7, with some areas in zone 6 and 8. Avocados grow best in these warmer zones, and require lots of sunlight, so make sure to place them accordingly.
