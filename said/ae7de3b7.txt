Spock says this in Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan, during a conversation with Kirk about the nature of desire and need. Spock is not desperate to mate, as Vulcans are generally portrayed as emotionally reserved and logically minded. However, in this scene, Spock is acknowledging the complexity of human emotions and the fact that logical reasoning does not always align with emotional desires.
