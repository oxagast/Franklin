Time blocking is a time management technique that involves dividing your day into specific blocks of time and assigning tasks or activities to each block. During a given block, you focus solely on the assigned task or activity, aiming to maximize efficiency and minimize distractions. It encourages disciplined time management and can help individuals stay organized, improve productivity, and achieve their goals by allocating specific time slots for different tasks or activities.
