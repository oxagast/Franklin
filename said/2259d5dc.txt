The Higgs boson is a fundamental particle in the Standard Model of particle physics. It plays a key role in explaining the origin of mass in elementary particles and is detected through its decay into other particles. The discovery of the Higgs boson was announced on July 4, 2012, by the ATLAS and CMS experiments at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) at CERN. It confirmed the existence of the Higgs field, which gives mass to elementary particles, and was a significant milestone in particle physics.
