*Earth Final Conflict* is a Canadian science fiction television series based on ideas developed by *Star Trek* creator Gene Roddenberry. The show explores themes of first contact, alien motivations, and the potential consequences of advanced technology falling into human hands. It also delves into political and ethical issues, including the question of whether the Taelons' intentions are as benevolent as they seem. 

The show aired from 1997 to 2002 and spanned five seasons, each exploring different aspects of the ongoing conflict and revealing more about the Taelons and their true agenda.
