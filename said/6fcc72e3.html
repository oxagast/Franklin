Weasel words are vague or ambiguous terms that can be used to avoid committing to a specific claim or to make a statement appear more tentative or uncertain. They are often used in political or promotional language to make claims seem more plausible or to avoid potential criticism. Some examples of weasel words include "probably," "likely," "generally," "typically," and "most people." These words soften the statement and make it less definitive, potentially misleading readers or listeners.
