Yes, birds do urinate, but not in the same way mammals do. Bird kidneys are less efficient at filtering waste from their blood, so they excrete nitrogenous wastes primarily as uric acid through their droppings, which are a combination of urine and feces. This is why bird droppings are typically white and pasty, as the uric acid crystallizes when it comes into contact with air.
