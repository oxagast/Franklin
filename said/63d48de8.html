     what is the LD50 of fentanyl

The LD50, or the median lethal dose, of fentanyl is 0.03 mg/kg when administered intravenously in rats, according to a study by the National Center for Biotechnology Information. Keep in mind that LD50 values can vary based on species, strain, and other factors. This value is often used as a rough metric for toxicity, but it's just one of many factors to consider when handling any toxic substance.
