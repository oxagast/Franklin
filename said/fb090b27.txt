Fungus growing inside your walls can potentially cause a variety of health issues. Here are some common symptoms associated with fungal growth in your home:

1. Respiratory Problems: Fungus spores can circulate in the air, leading to respiratory issues like coughing, wheezing, and difficulty breathing. This is especially problematic for individuals with asthma or other respiratory conditions.

2. Allergies and Irritation: Fungal growth can trigger allergies and irritation in some people. Symptoms may include sneezing, itching eyes, runny nose, and skin rashes. In some cases, these allergic reactions can be severe.

3. Mycotoxicosis: This condition occurs when toxic fungi produce harmful metabolites (mycotoxins) that can affect human health. Exposure to these toxins can lead to nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and in severe cases, even organ damage or failure.

4. Neurological Effects: Some types of fungi have been linked to neurological issues. This can range from headaches, dizziness, and fatigue to more severe symptoms like cognitive impairment and mood changes.

5. Immune System Compromise: Prolonged exposure to indoor fungi may suppress your immune system, making you more susceptible to various illnesses and infections.

6. Eye Irritation: Fungi can cause eye irritation, redness, and infections, especially in individuals who are sensitive to spores or mycotoxins.

7. Skin Problems: Direct contact with fungal growths can lead to skin infections, rashes, or irritations.

If you suspect fungal growth in your walls, it's advisable to take action to remediate the issue and consult a healthcare professional if you experience any of these symptoms. Fungal growth can sometimes be hidden and difficult to spot, so keeping a watchful eye and maintaining a healthy environment is crucial.
